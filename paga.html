The PAGA Story. Your own employees can sue on the state's behalf.

Since 2004, PAGA has let employees sue for themselves, their coworkers and the state. The 2024 reform changed the math: all reasonable steps taken before a notice can cap the penalties at 15%, and within 60 days after, at 30%. But the steps only help if you can show them.

The 15% and 30% caps are of the penalties sought and can apply where a court finds all reasonable steps were taken, weighing the totality of the circumstances; no outcome is guaranteed. Lab. Code §2699.3(c) lets employers with fewer than 100 employees in total cure certain violations — corrected, wages owed paid with 7% interest; the employee may not proceed on a violation the agency determines cured, subject to appeal.
