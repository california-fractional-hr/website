General information, not legal advice. Current as of 10.31.2026. Employer-size gates apply: paid sick leave at every employer, and local ordinances can require more · CFRA at 5+ employees · FMLA at 50+ employees within 75 miles. CFRA and FMLA also require 12 months of service and 1,250 hours in the prior 12 months.
