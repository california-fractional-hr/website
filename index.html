The demand engine

Three California rules that make HR help non-optional.

SB 553 · LABOR CODE 6401.9

Workplace violence prevention

A written plan, plus training at rollout, every year after, and on every change. A recurring, auditable obligation — the structural basis for a retainer.

RECURS ANNUALLY
SB 1162 · LABOR CODE 432.3

Pay-scale transparency

Employers with 15+ staff must publish salary ranges in every job posting. Every routine hire is now a compliance event.

$100–$10,000 PER VIOLATION
