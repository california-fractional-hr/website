Straight answers

Questions owners actually ask.

Is this a PEO?

No. No co-employment, no handing over your EIN, no giving up control of your culture. Independent advisory that answers to you — not a bundle you're locked into.

How does "fractional" actually work?

A set block of senior hours each month on retainer, plus project work as needed. Enough attention to run HR right — without a $150K+ full-time salary on the books.

Where do you work?

Based in Ventura County, on-site across Ventura and Santa Barbara. Virtual delivery extends the same work to California employers statewide.

Do you give legal advice?

No. HR and compliance strategy — not legal representation. The work runs alongside your employment counsel, and usually makes their job smaller.
